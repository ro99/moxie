## Decisions

- [ADR 0012](../decisions/adr/0012-explicit-family-operation-parameters.md):
  family mathematics is explicit operation parameters with no defaults. A
  defaulted parameter is invisible at the call site, which is where a reader
  looks to find out what a graph does.
- [ADR 0013](../decisions/adr/0013-one-model-crate-with-family-modules.md): one
  `moxie-models` crate with a module per family. **This reverses the contract's
  own plan**, at the owner's direction during implementation. The boundary is the
  dependency list, which a module cannot widen, so a crate per family bought
  ceremony rather than enforcement. The `moxie-models-*` prefix is retained for a
  family that genuinely needs its own dependency, and is still tested.
- No owner gate was resolved. O1–O7 remain open. No numerical threshold,
  precision, context target or compatibility surface changed.
- Two diagnostic bounds moved, both stated in the task record: a seventh
  parameter beyond the contract's six (the grouped norm, because per-head
  normalization cannot be composed from `Concat`/`Split` — those have no
  `OpParams` and no oracle), and the host-reference graph-value cap from 128 to
  384, which grows the reserved control envelope by at most 1 MiB. Context,
  chunk, tensor extent, rank and layer caps are unchanged.

## Remaining hypotheses and blockers

**M3 blocks the artifact, and nothing in this task substitutes for it.** Every
language-model linear is compressed-tensors INT8 `pack-quantized`, group 32,
symmetric, four codes per `int32` along the input axis, with BF16 scales. The
importer, packed-layout reader, canonical repack and W8A16 execution path are all
M3.

**M4 blocks the real graph's shape.** The artifact's sliding layers are 16 heads
of 256 and its global layers 4 of 512. One `KvGeometry` cannot hold both, and
sliding layers want ring eviction at the window rather than full retention. The
engine refuses a mixed-geometry graph rather than paging it at whichever width it
saw first, and a test asserts that refusal.

**M11 blocks vision.** The artifact is `image-text-to-text` and its mask rule has
a multimodal group exemption. The reduced graph is text-only.

What was checked and is not a blocker: the text equations are all recoverable
from the pinned legacy source, and the frozen constant table
`kGemma4ExecutionContract` matches the artifact's `config.json` field for field,
including the `(layer + 1) % 6 == 0` global predicate that exactly explains which
ten layers ship no `v_proj`.

One approach was rejected during implementation and is worth not repeating:
composing per-head normalization from `Concat`/`Split`. Those operations exist in
the `Op` enum but have no `OpParams` and no registered oracle, so a graph cannot
contain them. The grouped norm parameter is the alternative.
